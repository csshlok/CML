# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vault is for knowledge workers, researchers, developers, and other Windows users
who repeatedly need trustworthy AI context from their own documents, notes,
conversations, links, media, and codebases. They work inside a private library,
often over long sessions, and need to retrieve, organize, verify, and selectively
share context without repeatedly uploading their complete working set.

## Product Purpose

Vault is a local-first context management layer. It turns user-selected material
into durable, searchable evidence; returns grounded answers with inspectable
citations; understands repositories through Odin; and exposes only bounded,
approved context to external tools. Success means users can reuse their knowledge
confidently while retaining clear control over storage, trust, scope, and sharing.

## Brand Personality

Private, precise, and calm. Vault should feel dependable and direct: technically
capable without looking theatrical, security-conscious without being alarming,
and dense enough for serious work without becoming visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Vault should not resemble a crowded analytics dashboard, a decorative AI SaaS
landing page, a chat toy that hides its evidence, or a security console that
overwhelms routine work with warnings. Avoid ornamental effects, invented
affordances, opaque automation, and visual hierarchy that competes with the task.

## Design Principles

1. Start from evidence and keep consequential outputs traceable to their source.
2. Make scope, trust, background work, and external access visible and controllable.
3. Preserve native Windows expectations for windowing, keyboard use, focus, and recovery.
4. Reveal operational depth progressively while keeping common work immediate.
5. Prefer stable, honest state over optimistic UI that can drift from durable data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Every interactive control
must have a visible focus state and an accessible name. Status must not depend on
color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences, support zoom and narrow windows,
and keep destructive, security-sensitive, and recovery actions explicit.
