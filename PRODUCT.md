# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CML is for general second-brain users who want a private local memory workspace on Windows, not just for developers. They are collecting personal notes, files, links, screenshots, chat transcripts, synced folders, and other memory artifacts into one vault. They usually interact with the app while trying to retrieve context, organize related material, set up local AI capabilities, or inspect what the system has learned without giving up control of their data.

## Product Purpose

CML exists to turn a local vault of personal material into a usable context system. It ingests and indexes user-owned sources, clusters related material, exposes search, chat, map, and timeline views over the same vault, and can train verified local cluster experts when the machine and model setup support it. Success means the user can trust the app to keep data local, explain what is happening in plain language, and make retrieval, organization, and setup feel clear instead of technical.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, precise.

The voice should guide the user through setup, privacy, and recovery-sensitive moments without sounding mechanical or alarmist. The product should feel local-first, spatial, and quietly confident rather than flashy, AI-hyped, or dashboard-heavy.

## Anti-references

- Cyberpunk, terminal-first, neon, or heavy purple/blue gradient AI styling.
- Marketing-style landing-page theatrics inside the authenticated app.
- Generic ML dashboards that foreground internal model machinery over the user’s vault and tasks.
- Floating-card overload where every region becomes an isolated tile instead of one workspace.
- Optimistic or fake system language that hides degraded states, missing setup, or unfinished background work.

## Design Principles

- Keep the vault as the center of gravity. Search, chat, clusters, map, and timeline should feel like different views over one local memory system.
- Guide before exposing internals. Use user-facing language by default and reveal runtime, database, or model details only when the user asks for them.
- Be honest about local AI cost and readiness. Setup, model imports, memory search, and expert training must explain size, time, privacy, and degraded states plainly.
- Favor calm precision over spectacle. The interface should feel trustworthy for daily use, with clear hierarchy, low noise, and strong affordance consistency.
- Scale without collapsing. The experience and underlying behavior should remain understandable and efficient whether a vault has a handful of clusters or thousands of organized artifacts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public V1 should meet a solid desktop accessibility baseline with clear readable contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly navigation, and reduced-motion respect. Setup, recovery, lock-state, and diagnostics flows must avoid ambiguous wording and should help users understand the next safe action. The UI should remain usable on narrower desktop windows and should not rely on unscrollable full-height layouts for longer onboarding or settings content.
